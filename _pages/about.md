I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Secure Software Engineering group](https://www.hni.uni-paderborn.de/en/sse) of Prof. Eric Bodden at [Paderborn University](https://www.uni-paderborn.de/en/). I completed my PhD in computer science at Clausthal University of Technology in 2026, advised by Prof. Mohammad Ghafari.

My research uses **machine learning and LLMs to find security problems in software**, such as leaked secrets and security bug reports, and asks how far we can **trust** these AI models.

**Research interests:** Trustworthy AI · AI4SE · LLMs & Software Security · Adversarial ML · Usable Security · Empirical SE
